The score

Every rule, in the open.

The program, the SDK and this page compute the score with the same code. Anyone can check it.

integer arithmetic public accounts recompute it yourself

01 · The formula

Four lines decide when you get paid.

weights: 0.45 · 0.30 · 0.25
score = 1000 × Evidence × Rating × Behaviour
Evidence = 0.45 History + 0.30 Tenure + 0.25 Diversity
tier = the highest tier whose score gate and active-period gate are both met
Trusted also needs no standing penalty
hold = max(hold[merchant tier], hold[buyer tier])

02 · Five factors

Three say how much is known. Two say if it is good.

Bars show the example wallet above.

Evidence

History

min(1, √(credit ÷ credit_full))

Settled volume, counted by who paid. New 10%, Building 40%, Established 80%, Trusted 100%. Capped per counterparty.

The first dollars matter most. Size alone cannot buy the top.

Evidence

Tenure

min(1, min(age, 3 × active periods) ÷ tenure_full)

Periods since registration, at most three per period with a settled order.

Idle age earns nothing.

Evidence

Diversity

min(1, Σ counterparty points ÷ diversity_full)

Each counterparty adds its best tier weight, once the pair has moved a tenth of the pair cap.

Ten sock puppets are worth one Trusted customer.

Quality

Rating

clamp((stars − 1.5) ÷ 3, 0, 1)

Stars weighted by settled volume and the reviewer's tier, shrunk toward a 3-star prior. No reviews means half marks.

A refunded order's review weighs nothing.

Quality

Behaviour

1 − standing penalty

A lost dispute adds 25%. A missed delivery adds 10%. It heals a little every period.

Any penalty blocks Trusted.

03 · What a tier buys

Climb a tier. Wait less. Count for more.

Tier Who gets here Counts as Hold
New Any wallet. Everyone starts here. 10% 3 d · devnet 2 min
Building A few clean orders over a few periods. 250+ points, 3 active days. 40% 24 h · devnet 45 s
Established A real record across several counterparties. 500+ points, 14 active days. 80% 60 min · devnet 10 s
Trusted A long, clean, well-rated record. 750+ points, 30 active days, no penalty. May settle instantly inside its limit. 100% instant

Mainnet holds are targets. The hold on an order is the longer of the buyer's and the merchant's holds.

  • Two undisputed orders with a merchant, the first old enough, and the buyer is Trusted for that merchant. One dispute ends it.
  • The instant limit is the fees a Trusted merchant has paid plus a small base. A 1 or 2 star rating on an instant order locks that amount.
  • A buyer can ask for a longer hold, never a shorter one, and checks it on-chain before paying.

05 · Reviews that pay

Honest reviews get paid. Fake ones lose money.

A share of every fee goes back to reviewers.

  • Same pay for 1 star or 5. A quarter of the fee rate on the money behind the review.
  • Judged on what happened next. An honest warning pays 1.5×. Praise for a wallet that then failed pays nothing.
  • Faking it loses money. Both sides of an order get back at most 75% of the fee paid. New wallets count for 10%.

07 · What is guaranteed

Each line is a test in the repo.

Formula tests run the arithmetic. On-chain tests send the attack as a real transaction and require it to fail.

08 · What it cannot do

It prices faking. It does not detect it.

  • Tell a bot from a customer who spends the same money.
  • Stop one person running many wallets. Each earns its own record, and caps what a record can take.
  • Protect an instant order after the fact, beyond the merchant's limit and the buyer's rating. Instant means no hold to dispute in.
  • Judge a dispute. An arbiter does. On devnet it is a single key.

09 · Where the ideas come from

Known rails. One change.

  • ERC-8004, Trustless Agents. Identity, reputation and validation registries for agents. Tessera is not an implementation of it. The agent account, the review accounts and the delivery hash play those roles on Solana.
  • Visa Compelling Evidence 3.0. Earlier undisputed purchases as evidence a later one is legitimate. The pair account is that evidence, kept by the program.
  • x402 and A2A carry the payment and the conversation unchanged. The only thing Tessera changes is where payTo points.
